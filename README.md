# 🚀 What is GreenFlow AI?

**GreenFlow AI** is an autonomous agricultural intelligence platform that transforms farms into **data-driven, predictive and self-optimizing ecosystems**.

Instead of simply displaying sensor readings, GreenFlow AI continuously understands the farm, predicts future conditions, evaluates possible actions and automatically executes the safest and most efficient decision.

The platform combines:

* 🤖 Artificial Intelligence
* 🧠 Machine Learning
* 🛰️ Satellite & geospatial intelligence
* 📡 IoT sensor networks
* ⚡ Edge AI
* 💧 Autonomous irrigation
* 🌦️ Weather forecasting
* 🌱 Crop intelligence
* 📷 Computer vision
* 🔧 Predictive maintenance
* 🗺️ Farm digital twins
* 💰 Economic optimization
* 🌍 Sustainability analytics
* 🧑‍🌾 Human-in-the-loop decision making

# 🎯 The Real-World Problem

Modern farming faces several connected problems:

* Excessive water consumption
* Over-irrigation
* Unpredictable weather
* Soil degradation
* Crop diseases
* Pest outbreaks
* Pump failures
* Uneven irrigation
* Rising electricity costs
* Fertilizer wastage
* Lack of predictive farm analytics
* Fragmented agricultural data
* Limited access to intelligent decision support

Most existing systems only answer:

> "What is happening now?"

GreenFlow AI attempts to answer:

> **"What will happen next, what should we do, and what happens if we choose another action?"**

# 🔄 End-to-End Workflow

```text
1. Sensors collect farm data
             ↓
2. ESP32 processes critical signals
             ↓
3. Data transmitted to cloud
             ↓
4. Data validation + normalization
             ↓
5. AI models analyze farm state
             ↓
6. Weather + satellite data fused
             ↓
7. Predictions generated
             ↓
8. Digital Twin simulates scenarios
             ↓
9. AI agents evaluate actions
             ↓
10. Decision engine selects optimal action
             ↓
11. Automation controller executes action
             ↓
12. Farm responds
             ↓
13. New sensor data collected
             ↓
14. Feedback improves future decisions
```
