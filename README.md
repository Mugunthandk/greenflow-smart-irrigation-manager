# 🚀 Overview

**GreenFlow AI** is an advanced precision-agriculture platform designed to combine:

- 🤖 Artificial Intelligence
- 🧠 Machine Learning
- 📡 IoT sensor networks
- 💧 Smart irrigation
- 🌦️ Weather intelligence
- 🌱 Crop intelligence
- 🛰️ Geospatial & satellite analytics
- 📷 Computer vision
- 💰 Farm economics
- ⚙️ Automation
- 📊 Predictive analytics

into one unified farm intelligence ecosystem.

Traditional agriculture often depends on fixed irrigation schedules, manual observations, and fragmented information.

GreenFlow AI introduces an intelligent decision layer that continuously analyzes farm conditions and answers:

> **When should I irrigate?**
>
> **Where should I irrigate?**
>
> **How much water should I use?**
>
> **Can irrigation be delayed because rain is coming?**
>
> **Is my crop under stress?**
>
> **Is my pump behaving abnormally?**
>
> **What will my expected yield be?**
>
> **How can I reduce cost and improve productivity?**
